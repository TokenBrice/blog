These guides are the fastest way to navigate my long-form DeFi research archive. Each page gathers the core reads, concepts and historical cases for a specific topic. Pick a route, not a token to buy.

## New to DeFi?

Start with the [risk guide](/defi-risk/), then the [Stablecoin Map](/stablecoin-marauder-map/) and [swap basics](/swap-swamp/). Keep [DeFi](/glossary/defi/), [AMM](/glossary/amm/) and [liquidity pool](/glossary/liquidity-pool/) definitions handy. The interfaces in older tutorials are dated; the risks do not vanish with a redesign.

## Already deploying capital?

Read the [lending risk framework](/money-markets-risk/), compare [pegged-asset DEX designs](/pegged-assets-swap/) and [Curve versus Velodrome](/crv-vs-velo/), then dig into [veNFT infrastructure](/venft-infrastructure/). Ask who pays the yield, who can change the rules and how you get out.

[Beginner](/difficulty/beginner/) means fewer prerequisites, Intermediate connects the mechanisms, and [Expert](/difficulty/expert/) assumes you can follow protocol design and risk trade-offs: none of these labels means safe.
